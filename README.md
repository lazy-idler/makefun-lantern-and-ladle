**Sesame & Flame** is an original offline HTML5 Canvas midnight izakaya & wok bistro cooking game built for make.fun's *Make the best cooking game* bounty. Chop proteins and fresh bok choy, simmer noodles, jasmine rice, and rich miso stock under the copper kettle hood, toss sizzling shrimp and pork in the ember wok for Wok Hei bonuses, stage extra prep on the side tray, layer 10 combinable signature dishes on the assembly board, and serve VIP Critics and night guests across 5 escalating shifts with upgrades and procedural Web Audio SFX.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open `http://localhost:8080` in a desktop browser and play with mouse or keyboard (`1-8` pantry, `C` chop, `B` kettle, `F` wok toss, `Q` side tray, `Space` assemble, `Enter` serve, `X` sink, `Shift` dash, `H` 10-dish codex, `M` mute, `P` pause).
